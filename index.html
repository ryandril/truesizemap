The map you grew up with is wrong about size

On 4 September 2026 the UN General Assembly voted 164 to 1 to “Correct the Map”. It asks schools, governments and tech companies to use Equal Earth, a projection that draws every country at its true relative area, instead of Mercator, the 1569 navigation chart that stretches land near the poles. The vote is not binding, and Mercator stays where it belongs, on charts for navigation.

  • Africa is 13.9× the size of Greenland, and nearly as large as the United States, China, India and Europe put together.
  • The United States would cover about 31% of Africa.
  • Alaska is roughly three quarters the size of Mexico, not a third of the continent.
  • Greenland is slightly smaller than the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Try it: tap any country to lift a copy. Drag it anywhere and it re-sizes to what it would look like there. Zoom in for cities. Then flip to Equal Earth and watch the distortion vanish.
