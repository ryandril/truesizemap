The True Size Map

The UN just voted 164–1 to retire this map.

On 4 September 2026 the UN General Assembly adopted the “Correct the Map” resolution, 164 votes to 1. It asks schools, governments and tech companies to use Equal Earth, a projection where every country is drawn at its true relative area, instead of Mercator, the 1569 navigation chart that stretches land near the poles. On Mercator, Greenland looks the size of Africa. Africa is 14 times bigger.

Try it: tap any country to lift a copy. Drag it anywhere and it re-sizes to what it would look like there. Flick it and it coasts. Then flip to Equal Earth and watch the distortion vanish.
